# SBI2 2025 Abstract Submission - CytoDataFrame

## Authors

Dave Bunten, Jenna Tomkinson, Gregory Way

## Title (under 100 characters)

Explore Single‑Cell Images and Measurements Together with CytoDataFrame

## Abstract (under 500 words)

Image‑based profiling involves the analysis of vast tables of measurements alongside thousands of cell snapshots.
Context-switching between tables or spreadsheets of feature data and image viewers makes it challenging to see what really matters.
CytoDataFrame solves this by bringing each cell or compartment object picture and feature data into one interactive table for Jupyter notebook environments.

With CytoDataFrame you can:

- Keep context at a glance. Every row shows both the numeric features (size, shape, intensity) and a thumbnail of the same cell, so you immediately know what the data represent.
- Adjust on the fly. Brightness sliders and mask toggles let you highlight segmentation errors or unusual cells without writing extra code.
- Filter and explore. Select subpopulations such as high‑intensity outliers, specific phenotypes, or quality‑control failures and viewing images and numbers together.
- Stay in your workflow. Extending Pandas DataFrames, it works with your favorite analysis and plotting commands, and exports results for downstream sharing or publication.

By unifying images and measurements in a single view, CytoDataFrame accelerates troubleshooting, improves data quality checks, and helps teams spot biological patterns faster.
It makes your next discovery just a click away, perfect for anyone doing high‑content imaging.
